Fair play

Your cards. Nobody else's.

RunGood reads your own cards, the board, and opponents' cards only once they are shown at showdown. Never hidden cards. It never changes or controls your poker client, and your hands stay on your own PC.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which poker sites does RunGood work with?

RunGood is built for ClubGG cash games today. More poker apps are planned.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. RunGood runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Do I have to import hand histories?

No. RunGood picks up every hand on its own while you play.

Where are my hands stored?

On your own PC. You can add automatic backups to any folder you like.

I used Club Tracker. What happens to my data?

RunGood is the new name for Club Tracker. It updates itself and keeps all your hands, settings and nicknames.

Does it update itself?

Yes. New versions install on their own the next time you open RunGood.
