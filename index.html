HUD

Know every regular in your club.

RunGood puts each opponent's numbers right next to their seat, in the same boxes you see in the app. Fish light up, regulars get a tag, and every number shows how many hands it's based on.

  • Stats per player, right on the table
  • Click-through, never in your way
  • Build your own boxes in the HUD designer

Graphs

Your graph, drawn live.

Every session drawn hand by hand, in big blinds or real money. Watch the line move while you're still sitting at the table.

Clubs

Fair play

Your cards. Nobody else's.

RunGood reads your own cards, the board, and opponents' cards only once they are shown at showdown. Never hidden cards. It never changes or controls your poker app, and your hands stay on your own PC.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which club apps does RunGood work with?

ClubGG cash games today. We are adding more club apps, so the same tracker covers all the clubs you play.

My club plays in chips. Does that work?

Yes. Set what a chip is worth in each club, and RunGood shows your results in real money.

Can my backer see my results?

Yes. RunGood makes a report for your backer as a page or an image, with your results, graph, sessions and settlements. An invite so your backer can follow your sessions live is coming soon.

Is RunGood free?

Yes, free for players during the beta. It is a beta, so some things may still change or break; you can report problems right from the app.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. RunGood runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Do I have to import hand histories?

No. RunGood picks up every hand on its own while you play.

Where are my hands stored?

On your own PC. Turn on automatic daily backups to any folder you like.

I used Club Tracker. What happens to my data?

RunGood is the new name for Club Tracker. It updates itself and keeps all your hands, settings and nicknames.
